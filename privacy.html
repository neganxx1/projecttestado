Account Rules

Privacy policy

Account data

When you sign in by email, EVU CORE uses your email address to send a sign-in link and identify your account. If you sign in with Google, Supabase processes the Google account identifier, email address and basic profile information authorized on the consent screen to create or sign you in to your EVU CORE account.

How account data is used

EVU CORE uses authentication data for account access, session management and support. Your account is managed by Supabase Auth. The browser stores a sign-in session for this site so you can stay signed in. Signing out ends the session in this browser.

Orders and support

Information you provide through the store or support channels is used to process your order, verify payment and delivery, provide support and communicate about the service. The checkout platform and its payment providers process order and payment data under their own policies.

Questions and account requests

Contact EVU CORE through the support address on the Google consent screen to ask about your data or request correction or deletion. Requests are subject to applicable legal and order requirements.
