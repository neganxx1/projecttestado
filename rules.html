Store rules

Purchases & conduct

  • You must be 18+ or have guardian permission to purchase.
  • Treat support and other buyers with respect. Harassment = permanent ban, no refund.
  • Impersonating staff, resellers, or other users is strictly forbidden.
  • Chargebacks or payment disputes result in a permanent ban and blacklist.

License Key Rules

  • Each key is bound to 1 device (HWID) on first activation. No sharing.
  • Sharing, leaking, reselling, or posting your key anywhere → instant revoke, no refund, permanent ban.
  • Attempting to bypass HWID lock, spoof HWID, or use the same key on multiple machines → immediate termination.
  • HWID reset is available on your dashboard for 50% of the original price, per your plan.
  • Keys are personal and non-transferable.

These rules are subject to applicable law and the store’s refund policy.
